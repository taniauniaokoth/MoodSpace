The Idea

Mood Space was created to help individuals become more aware of their emotions. Many people go through their day without reflecting on how they feel.

The Problem

People often struggle to track their emotions consistently, making it harder to identify patterns, triggers, and growth over time.

The Solution

This application provides a simple and intuitive way to log moods, store them, and revisit them later — creating a personal emotional timeline.

Our Vision

To create a safe digital space where people can understand themselves without judgment.

Core Features

  • Mood logging
  • Personal notes
  • History tracking
  • Simple and calming interface
